## FALSE GATE 1: Interest Gate

### Proposal Claim
"Interest Gate — authority over accounting, interest tracking, conflict detection, and financial audits."

### REFUSAL — REJECTED ✗

**Reason:** Interest is an **audit function**, not a gate. Gates control passage and progression. Interest audits *examine* passage but do not *permit* it.

### Correct Authority
**Usury Ledger** (appointed by Chief Architect, governed by Governance Lead)

**Usury Ledger Mandate:**
- Tracks internal conflicts and predation within and between projects
- Records interest disputes (cost inflation, schedule slippage, resource hoarding)
- Issues audit verdicts (conflict resolved, further investigation required, escalation to executive)
- Maintains immutable audit trail
- **Does NOT issue passage permits. Does NOT own gates.**

### Why Interest ≠ Gate

| Aspect | Gate | Interest Audit |
|--------|------|---|
| **Controls passage?** | YES — stops or allows progression | NO — examines after passage |
| **Issues ALIVE verdict?** | YES — gates emit ALIVE/PARTIAL/BLOCKED | NO — Usury Ledger audits existing decisions |
| **Owns projects?** | YES — primary gate owns the project | NO — audit is secondary/oversight |
| **Immutable receipts?** | YES — gate receipt is final | NO — audit trail is amendable for new evidence |

**Outcome:** Interest Gate **REJECTED**. Usury Ledger **ACCEPTED** as the correct authority for conflict tracking.
